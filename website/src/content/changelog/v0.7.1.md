## Added

- Remove and clear-history operations that preserve completed files.
- Repeated-URL downloads, clean retry with a new ID, and validated canceled-download resume.
- Configurable completed-download directory with a `$HOME/Downloads` default.
- Collision-safe filenames and cross-filesystem finalization.

## Fixed

- Partial transfers now live under XDG state instead of the CLI working directory.
- Interactive watch redraws in place while piped watch emits one snapshot.
- Smoothed and debounced ETA avoids reacting directly to every throughput sample.
- TUI hierarchy, scrolling, narrow layouts, confirmations, lifecycle actions, and help were stabilized.
