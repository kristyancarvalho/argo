## Added

- The narrowly authorized `argo-qosd` helper with scoped Linux capabilities.
- Argo-owned nftables classification and conflict-aware HTB plus `fq_codel` shaping.
- `off`, `balanced`, `throughput`, and `focus` policies through `argo policy`.
- Policy reconciliation across disconnects, interface transitions, and helper outages.

## Security

- The main download daemon remains unprivileged; kernel changes cross a constrained typed IPC boundary.
