## Added

- `argo tui` as a daemon-backed terminal application shell.
- A selectable download list with progress, speed, ETA, status, and priority.
- Download add, pause, resume, cancel, and priority actions.
- Network, profile, QoS, adaptive rate, and latency views.
- Policy and profile controls that surface helper failures without exiting.

## Changed

- End-to-end, race, shutdown, migration, scheduler, network, QoS, adaptive, and TUI coverage completed the original roadmap.
