## Added

- Per-transfer and aggregate Argo throughput telemetry.
- An optional bounded TCP-connect latency probe and stable expiring baseline.
- Gradual adaptive shaping within configured minimum and maximum rates.
- The `latency` policy with safe missing-telemetry behavior.
- Adaptive profile settings and status diagnostics.
- A Makefile with local CI-equivalent targets.
